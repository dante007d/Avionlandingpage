Topic Auction Challenge

BID SMART, WIN BIG

A team-based technical competition where participants use virtual coins to bid on Easy, Medium and Hard technical topics across three auction rounds. Based on their selected topics, teams solve three question papers and earn points through accuracy and speed. The team with the highest score emerges as the winner.

  • Strategic use of virtual coins
  • Teamwork and decision-making
  • Technical problem-solving challenges
  • Accuracy-based scoring and speed bonus
